# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis 🛒📊

## Overview
This repository contains an end-to-end data analytics project examining **3,900 e-commerce transactions** across multiple product categories. The goal of the project is to evaluate customer spending patterns, subscription drivers, product popularity, and demographic segments to deliver actionable business strategies. 

The workflow spans the complete data lifecycle:
1. **Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** using **Python (Pandas)**
2. **Relational Database Storage & Advanced Business Queries** using **PostgreSQL**
3. **Interactive Visualization & Executive Reporting** using **Power BI**

---

## Dataset Overview
- **Records:** 3,900 transactions
- **Attributes:** 18 features capturing customer profiles, purchase metrics, and behavior
- **Key Columns:** `Age`, `Gender`, `Location`, `Item Purchased`, `Category`, `Purchase Amount (USD)`, `Subscription Status`, `Review Rating`, `Shipping Type`, `Discount Applied`, `Previous Purchases`

---

## Tech Stack & Tools
- **Language:** Python 
- **Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, SQLAlchemy
- **Database:** MySQL
- **Visualization:** Power BI Desktop

---

## Technical Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation & EDA (Python)
* **Missing Value Imputation:** Handled missing values in `Review Rating` (37 instances) using median imputation grouped by product category.
* **Schema Standardization:** Normalized column names to `snake_case` for SQL compatibility.
* **Feature Engineering:**
  * Created `age_group` bins (`Young Adult`, `Middle-aged`, `Adult`, `Senior`).
  * Engineered purchase frequency metrics.
* **Redundancy Cleanup:** Validated correlation between `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used` to drop redundant columns.
* **Pipeline Integration:** Connected Python directly to PostgreSQL to automate database ingestion.

### 2. SQL Analysis (MySQL)
Key queries were designed to extract strategic business insights:
* **Revenue Segmentation:** Analyzed total revenue generated by gender and age group.
* **Subscription Mechanics:** Evaluated spending behavior and order volumes between subscribers and non-subscribers.
* **Discount Elasticity:** Identified high-spending customers utilizing discounts, as well as products with high discount dependency.
* **Customer Lifecycle:** Classified users into `New`, `Returning`, and `Loyal` segments based on historical purchase frequency.
* **Category Performance:** Ranked top-performing products within each category using window functions (`RANK()`).

### 3. Business Intelligence Dashboard (Power BI)
Built an interactive dashboard featuring:
* **KPI Cards:** Total Customers ($3.9\text{K}$), Average Purchase Amount ($\$59.76$), and Average Review Rating ($3.75$).
* **Demographic Breakdown:** Revenue and order volume sliced by age group and gender.
* **Sales Distribution:** Revenue vs. unit sales across major product categories.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filtering by Shipping Type, Subscription Status, and Product Category.

---

## Key Findings & Business Insights
* **Revenue by Gender:** Male shoppers accounted for approximately $67.7\%$ of total revenue ($\$157,890$ vs. $\$75,191$).
* **Subscription Gap:** Only $27\%$ of customers are subscribers. However, average spend per transaction between subscribers ($\$59.49$) and non-subscribers ($\$59.87$) remains nearly identical.
* **Discount Sensitivity:** Items like **Hats** ($50.0\%$), **Sneakers** ($49.66\%$), and **Coats** ($49.07\%$) rely heavily on promotional discounts.
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** **Young Adults** generate the highest overall revenue ($\$62,143$), followed closely by **Middle-aged** buyers ($\$59,197$).

---

## Strategic Recommendations
1. **Drive Subscription Value:** Offer exclusive perks (e.g., free shipping, early access to sales) to convert non-subscribers into recurring members.
2. **Loyalty Optimization:** Implement reward tiers for repeat buyers to shift "Returning" customers into the "Loyal" segment.
3. **Margin Management:** Restructure promotional strategies on high-discount items (e.g., Hats, Sneakers) to protect profit margins.
4. **Targeted Campaigns:** Focus marketing budget on high-spending age brackets and express-shipping preference segments.
