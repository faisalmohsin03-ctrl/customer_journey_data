# Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis 🛒📊

## Overview
This repository contains an end-to-end data analytics project examining **3,900 e-commerce transactions** across multiple product categories[cite: 1]. The goal of the project is to evaluate customer spending patterns, subscription drivers, product popularity, and demographic segments to deliver actionable business strategies[cite: 1]. 

The workflow spans the complete data lifecycle:
1. **Data Cleaning & Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** using **Python (Pandas)**[cite: 1].
2. **Relational Database Storage & Advanced Business Queries** using **PostgreSQL**[cite: 1].
3. **Interactive Visualization & Executive Reporting** using **Power BI**[cite: 1].

---

## Dataset Overview
- **Records:** 3,900 transactions[cite: 1]
- **Attributes:** 18 features capturing customer profiles, purchase metrics, and behavior[cite: 1]
- **Key Columns:** `Age`, `Gender`, `Location`, `Item Purchased`, `Category`, `Purchase Amount (USD)`, `Subscription Status`, `Review Rating`, `Shipping Type`, `Discount Applied`, `Previous Purchases`[cite: 1]

---

## Tech Stack & Tools
- **Language:** Python 3.x[cite: 1]
- **Libraries:** Pandas, NumPy, SQLAlchemy / psycopg2[cite: 1]
- **Database:** PostgreSQL[cite: 1]
- **Visualization:** Power BI Desktop[cite: 1]

---

## Technical Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation & EDA (Python)
* **Missing Value Imputation:** Handled missing values in `Review Rating` (37 instances) using median imputation grouped by product category[cite: 1].
* **Schema Standardization:** Normalized column names to `snake_case` for SQL compatibility[cite: 1].
* **Feature Engineering:**
  * Created `age_group` bins (`Young Adult`, `Middle-aged`, `Adult`, `Senior`)[cite: 1].
  * Engineered purchase frequency metrics[cite: 1].
* **Redundancy Cleanup:** Validated correlation between `discount_applied` and `promo_code_used` to drop redundant columns[cite: 1].
* **Pipeline Integration:** Connected Python directly to PostgreSQL to automate database ingestion[cite: 1].

### 2. SQL Analysis (PostgreSQL)
Key queries were designed to extract strategic business insights:
* **Revenue Segmentation:** Analyzed total revenue generated by gender and age group[cite: 1].
* **Subscription Mechanics:** Evaluated spending behavior and order volumes between subscribers and non-subscribers[cite: 1].
* **Discount Elasticity:** Identified high-spending customers utilizing discounts, as well as products with high discount dependency[cite: 1].
* **Customer Lifecycle:** Classified users into `New`, `Returning`, and `Loyal` segments based on historical purchase frequency[cite: 1].
* **Category Performance:** Ranked top-performing products within each category using window functions (`RANK()`)[cite: 1].

### 3. Business Intelligence Dashboard (Power BI)
Built an interactive dashboard featuring[cite: 1]:
* **KPI Cards:** Total Customers ($3.9\text{K}$), Average Purchase Amount ($\$59.76$), and Average Review Rating ($3.75$)[cite: 1].
* **Demographic Breakdown:** Revenue and order volume sliced by age group and gender[cite: 1].
* **Sales Distribution:** Revenue vs. unit sales across major product categories[cite: 1].
* **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filtering by Shipping Type, Subscription Status, and Product Category[cite: 1].

---

## Key Findings & Business Insights
* **Revenue by Gender:** Male shoppers accounted for approximately $67.7\%$ of total revenue ($\$157,890$ vs. $\$75,191$)[cite: 1].
* **Subscription Gap:** Only $27\%$ of customers are subscribers[cite: 1]. However, average spend per transaction between subscribers ($\$59.49$) and non-subscribers ($\$59.87$) remains nearly identical[cite: 1].
* **Discount Sensitivity:** Items like **Hats** ($50.0\%$), **Sneakers** ($49.66\%$), and **Coats** ($49.07\%$) rely heavily on promotional discounts[cite: 1].
* **Top Revenue Drivers:** **Young Adults** generate the highest overall revenue ($\$62,143$), followed closely by **Middle-aged** buyers ($\$59,197$)[cite: 1].

---

## Strategic Recommendations
1. **Drive Subscription Value:** Offer exclusive perks (e.g., free shipping, early access to sales) to convert non-subscribers into recurring members[cite: 1].
2. **Loyalty Optimization:** Implement reward tiers for repeat buyers to shift "Returning" customers into the "Loyal" segment[cite: 1].
3. **Margin Management:** Restructure promotional strategies on high-discount items (e.g., Hats, Sneakers) to protect profit margins[cite: 1].
4. **Targeted Campaigns:** Focus marketing budget on high-spending age brackets and express-shipping preference segments[cite: 1].
